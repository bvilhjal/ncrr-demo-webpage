Danish registers

A population followed from cradle to grave — a unique research resource.

Home › Danish registers

Register data covering the entire Danish population have been gathered over decades, largely for administrative purposes, from cradle to grave. Danish legislation permits researchers to use this data for research of general relevance and importance.

Drawing on these registers, NCRR researchers provide expertise in all areas of population-based epidemiology, including legislative and ethical aspects of handling data, data management, security, and the implementation of epidemiological research. By appointment, NCRR staff offer advisory and practical support for register-based research and welcome collaboration with Danish and international colleagues.
